Privacy Policy

Jamia Masjid Mohi-Ul-Islam Siddiqia · 124 Barking Road, London E6 3BD

Summary

This website collects no personal data. Nothing you do here is stored on our servers.

1. Who we are

This privacy policy applies to the website of Jamia Masjid Mohi-Ul-Islam Siddiqia, 124 Barking Road, London E6 3BD ("we", "the mosque"). For any privacy question, contact the mosque office directly using the details on our Contact page.

2. Information we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers or addresses of visitors.
  • We do not use advertising networks or third-party trackers.
  • We do not create profiles, and we do not sell or share anything — because we hold nothing to sell.

3. Contact form

The contact form does not send data to this website. It simply opens your own email application with the message pre-filled. The email is sent by your email provider to the mosque's email address, outside of this website entirely.

4. Cookies and local storage

We use only essential local storage on your device to remember your preferences:

  • Theme choice (light / dark)
  • Language choice (English / Urdu / Bangla)
  • Your cookie-consent choice, and whether you have accepted the Terms
  • Which announcements you have already seen, so pop-ups do not repeat

These never leave your device. You can clear them anytime via your browser settings, or via "Cookie Preferences" in the website footer.

5. Prayer times and content

Prayer times and site content are managed by the mosque and loaded from the mosque's own administration service. These requests contain no personal data.

6. Third-party content

The daily hadith is fetched from a public, key-free open dataset (jsDelivr CDN). Requests contain no personal data, identifiers or cookies — only a public hadith number.

The location map is an embedded Google Maps frame showing the mosque's public address. Google may process technical data per its own policies when the map loads.

Donation buttons lead to external payment providers. We never see or store card details; please review the provider's own privacy terms before donating.

7. Your rights (UK GDPR)

Because this website processes no personal data, most data rights are not engaged. If you believe otherwise, or for anything else, contact the mosque directly and we will help.

8. Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published on this page.
