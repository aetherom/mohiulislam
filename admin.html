Help & Guide
  • Saving: Save draft stores edits privately — nothing goes live until gold Publish. Revert restores the last saved state, so mistakes are always undoable before publishing.
  • Monthly times: pick Year then Month in the sidebar. Times are 24-hour — type digits or click the small 🕐 and spin the wheels. ⤓ copies the day above · Copy from… pulls another month & year in.
  • Today: with unsaved edits, a popup offers to save before jumping — nothing is lost.
  • Jumu'ah: From date → To date with Jamat 1 & 2. Extra jamats go in Extra Jamats.
  • Ramadan: date range → Suhoor ends & Iftar per day (Fill-all and ⤓ included). The 🌙 Duas button edits the duas shown under the timings.
  • Appearance: the Dark mode toggle lives in Site Settings — it styles the entire panel and is remembered on this device.
